# Project Truth

## Project Identity

Szymik Digital is a lean digital studio for practical business websites, local visibility, and useful digital systems.

It is being built as the commercial umbrella for:

- Local business websites
- Client support and improvement work
- Local SEO foundations
- Automation and custom digital tools
- Future owned products such as CoreShift and other apps

## One-Line Positioning

Szymik Digital helps small businesses get practical websites, local visibility, and digital systems that make day-to-day work easier.

## Core Mission

Help small businesses become easier to find, easier to trust, and easier to run online.

The focus is not design for its own sake. The focus is commercial usefulness: clearer websites, better local search foundations, smoother client enquiries, and simple systems that reduce manual work.

## What Szymik Digital Does

- Builds and improves small business websites
- Creates local SEO foundations, including service pages, location relevance, basic technical hygiene, and Google Business Profile support where appropriate
- Fixes unclear, outdated, slow, or underperforming websites
- Builds practical landing pages and service pages
- Helps businesses present their offer more clearly
- Provides monthly website support and small improvement work
- Designs simple automations and custom tools for repeated admin, client handling, reporting, or operational workflows
- Develops future owned products where a repeated client problem becomes product-shaped

## What Szymik Digital Does Not Do

- Operate as a generic web design agency
- Sell visual design detached from business outcomes
- Chase large agency-style retainers before the offer is proven
- Promise overnight SEO rankings or vague growth guarantees
- Act as a social media content agency
- Take on complex enterprise transformation work
- Build custom software without a clear business problem and scope
- Add technology for novelty rather than usefulness

## Target Clients

Primary clients are small, local, service-led businesses that need a stronger digital foundation but do not need a large agency.

Good-fit clients include:

- Local care and support providers
- Trades and home services
- Clinics, wellness providers, and appointment-led businesses
- Consultants and professional service providers
- Small teams with outdated or unclear websites
- Businesses that rely on local trust, referrals, and search visibility

The best-fit client wants a practical partner who can make their online presence clearer, more credible, and easier to maintain.

## Core Commercial Thesis

Small businesses often do not need a big agency. They need someone who can identify the most useful digital improvements, build them cleanly, and support them without making the process heavy.

Szymik Digital should grow from a focused service wedge:

1. Build trust through websites and local visibility.
2. Add recurring value through support and improvements.
3. Identify repeated operational problems.
4. Turn repeatable problems into automation tools and, where justified, owned products.

This creates a path from service revenue to product insight without forcing a SaaS business too early.

## Relationship To Libra Support Services

The Libra Support Services website should be treated as practical proof of the Szymik Digital approach.

It can demonstrate:

- Clear positioning for a real local service business
- Service-led website structure
- Trust-building content
- Local-business friendly language
- A useful foundation for future local visibility work

Future-facing note: if permission and suitable details are available, Libra Support Services can become the first formal case study for Szymik Digital.

## Relationship To CoreShift And Future Products

CoreShift and other future apps should sit under the broader Szymik Digital commercial umbrella.

Current priority is not to lead with SaaS. The first commercial wedge is service-led: websites, local visibility, client support, and practical systems.

Future-facing product direction:

- Use client work to identify repeated problems
- Build simple internal tools or client-specific automations first
- Turn repeated, validated patterns into owned products only when the demand is clear
- Position products as practical extensions of the same mission: making small businesses easier to run

## Brand Voice

Szymik Digital should sound:

- Practical
- Clear
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Local-business friendly
- Commercially aware without being pushy

Avoid agency jargon, over-polished hype, vague transformation language, and technical showing off.

## Current Strategic Wedge

The current wedge is:

1. Websites and local visibility first
2. Automation and custom systems second

This order matters. Websites and local search foundations are easier for small businesses to understand and buy. Automation and custom tools become stronger once trust exists and real operational problems are visible.
