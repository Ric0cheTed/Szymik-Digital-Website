# What I would do differently

## 1. I would stop treating the website as the “main thing”

The website matters, but the **offer** matters more.

A lot of beginners build a nice agency website and then sit there waiting. That is the slow lane.

I’d build the Szymik Digital site around **clear conversion paths**:

* “I need a website”
* “I need help getting found locally”
* “I need my current website improved”
* “I need automation or a custom tool”

Each path should lead to a simple enquiry form or call request.

No waffle. No corporate fog machine.

# What I would avoid

## Avoid building a massive agency site too early

You do not need a 30-page monster.

You need a site that gets someone to say:

> “This guy can probably sort my website out.”

That is the job.

---

## Avoid selling too many services at once

You can do websites, SaaS, bots, AI tools, dashboards, games, automation, SEO, content systems…

But the public offer should be simpler.

For clients, I’d lead with:

> Websites and digital systems for small businesses.

Then expand during the conversation.

---

## Avoid undercharging just because you are still learning

You can start with fair starter pricing, but do not race to the bottom.

The Libra site alone gives you a proper example of modern, structured, SEO-aware work.

You are past “£100 website mate” territory. Leave that swamp for the frogs.
