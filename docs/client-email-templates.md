# Client Email Templates

These templates are starting points. Keep the tone warm, clear, and practical. Adjust each message so it fits the client and the project.

## Enquiry Received

Subject: Thanks for getting in touch, [Client Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for getting in touch with Szymik Digital about [Project Type] for [Business Name].

We have received your enquiry and will take a look at the details. If we need anything else before Ric reviews it properly, we will come back to you with a few questions.

Best,  
[Hannah Name]

## More Information Needed

Subject: A few details about [Business Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for your enquiry. Before Ric reviews the next step, could you send over a little more detail?

- What are you hoping to improve or build?
- Do you already have a website? If yes, please send the link.
- Is there a deadline we should know about?
- Are there any examples, issues, or goals you want us to understand?

Once we have that, we can point you in the right direction.

Best,  
[Hannah Name]

## Discovery Questions

Subject: Discovery questions for [Business Name]

Hi [Client Name],

To help us understand the right scope for [Project Type], could you answer the questions below?

- What does [Business Name] do?
- Who are your main customers?
- What services or offers matter most?
- What is not working with the current website or digital setup?
- What outcome would make this project worthwhile?
- Who will provide copy, images, logos, and business details?
- Is there a budget range or deadline we should consider?
- Who will approve the work?

Short answers are fine. We just need enough to make the next step useful.

Best,  
[Hannah Name]
