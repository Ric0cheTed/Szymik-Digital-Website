# Quote, Invoice, and Client Approval Pack

This pack gives Ric and Hannah practical wording for quotes, invoices, payment requests, scope changes, and written approvals from quote through to launch.

Use this as operational guidance for Szymik Digital. It is not legal or accounting advice. Final pricing, tax treatment, payment records, and contractual wording should be checked by the appropriate professional if needed.

## Usage Guide

- Hannah can send and coordinate client-facing admin messages.
- Ric confirms technical scope, price, timeline, and build approach.
- Do not start paid work until the client has approved the quote and deposit/payment terms are met.
- Keep approval wording written, explicit, and easy to find later.
- Use the client tracker to record quote status, payment status, and approval status.
- Keep one clear next action in every message.
- Avoid promising launch dates before Ric has confirmed technical readiness.
- If a request changes the agreed scope, pause and confirm whether it needs a separate quote.

## Suggested Quote Sections

### Project Summary

Keep this short and plain. Explain what the project is, what the client needs, and the outcome Szymik Digital is aiming to deliver.

Example:

```text
Szymik Digital will create a [Project Type] for [Business Name] to help the business present its services clearly, improve trust with local customers, and provide a simple route for enquiries.
```

### What Is Included

List exactly what is being delivered. Use bullets so the client can scan it quickly.

Useful inclusions:

- Number of pages or page types
- Design/build work
- Copy editing or copy placement if included
- Contact details and enquiry route
- Basic mobile checks
- Basic SEO setup if included
- Launch support if included
- Agreed revision rounds
- Handover notes

### What Is Not Included

Be clear without sounding defensive. This protects Ric, Hannah, and the client from assumptions.

Useful exclusions:

- New branding or logo design unless agreed
- Professional copywriting unless agreed
- New photography, video, or illustration
- Large content rewrites
- Extra pages, features, booking systems, shops, memberships, or portals
- CRM setup or marketing automation
- Ongoing maintenance unless agreed
- Paid third-party tools, hosting, domains, email, plugins, or software licences

### Content / Assets Needed From Client

State what the client must provide before or during the project.

Useful items:

- Logo files
- Brand colours or brand guide if available
- Photos or approved images
- Business description
- Service descriptions
- Contact details
- Reviews/testimonials
- Domain, hosting, or website access if Ric confirms it is needed
- Any legal, privacy, cookie, or policy wording the client is responsible for

### Timeline Assumptions

Use timelines as assumptions, not promises, until Ric confirms the schedule.

Starter wording:

```text
The timeline depends on timely client approval, content, assets, feedback, and any access needed for the build. Ric will confirm the working schedule once the quote is approved and the deposit/payment terms are met.
```

### Amendment / Revision Rounds

State how feedback works before the client approves the quote.

Starter wording:

```text
This quote includes [Number] revision round(s) on the agreed scope. Feedback should be sent in one clear list where possible. New pages, new features, major redesigns, or substantial content rewrites may be quoted separately.
```

### Third-Party Costs

Third-party costs should not be hidden inside vague wording.

Starter wording:

```text
Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, email, stock images, plugins, subscriptions, or paid tools are not included unless listed above. These may need to be paid directly by the client or invoiced before purchase.
```

### Hosting / Domain / Email Responsibilities

Keep ownership clear.

Starter wording:

```text
The client remains responsible for domain, hosting, and email accounts unless a separate support agreement says otherwise. Szymik Digital can advise on setup or changes where this is included in the agreed scope.
```

### Payment Terms

Make the payment structure easy to understand.

Starter wording:

```text
Payment terms are [Deposit Amount] before work starts and [Final Balance] before launch or handover. Work begins once the quote is approved and the deposit/payment terms are met.
```

### Approval Instruction

Tell the client exactly how to approve.

Starter wording:

```text
To approve this quote, please reply by email with: "I approve the quote for [Project Type] for [Business Name] and agree to the payment terms."
```

## Payment Terms Wording

Use this as starter wording in quotes, invoices, and admin emails.

```text
For most fixed-price projects, a 50% deposit is required before work starts. The remaining balance is due before launch or handover.

Smaller fixed jobs may require full payment upfront.

Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, email, stock images, plugins, subscriptions, or paid tools may need to be paid separately by the client or invoiced before purchase.

Additional work outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately before it begins.
```

## Scope Change Handling

Use these when a client asks for something outside the agreed quote. Keep the tone helpful, but make the boundary clear.

### Small Extra Request

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for sending this over. This looks like a small extra request outside the agreed quote for [Project Type].

I will check with Ric whether this can be included within the current revision round or whether it needs a small additional quote.

Next action: I will confirm the best route before any extra work is started.

Best,
[Hannah Name]
```

### Larger Change Needs Separate Quote

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for explaining the change you would like.

This looks larger than the agreed scope for [Project Type], so we would need to quote it separately before Ric starts that work. That keeps the current project clear and avoids any surprise costs.

If you would like us to price it, I can ask Ric to review the request and come back with the next step.

Next action: please confirm if you would like a separate quote for this change.

Best,
[Hannah Name]
```

### New Phase After Launch

```text
Hi [Client Name],

That sounds like a useful next step for [Business Name].

Because it is separate from the agreed launch scope, the cleanest route is to treat it as a new phase after launch. We can complete the current project first, then Ric can review and quote the next phase properly.

Next action: once launch is complete, I can help gather the details for Ric to review.

Best,
[Hannah Name]
```

### Client Asks For Something Not Included

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for asking about this.

That item is not included in the current quote for [Project Type]. Ric can review whether it is something Szymik Digital can help with, and if so, we can confirm the scope and price before any work begins.

Next action: please confirm whether you would like Ric to review this as an additional request.

Best,
[Hannah Name]
```

## Revision / Amendment Rules

- Reasonable amendments are included only within the agreed revision rounds.
- Feedback should be sent in one clear list where possible.
- Feedback should relate to the agreed scope, pages, content, and functionality.
- New features, new pages, major redesigns, or substantial content rewrites may be quoted separately.
- If feedback is unclear, Hannah should ask for clarification before Ric starts changes.
- If a request may affect timeline, price, or technical approach, Ric should confirm before Hannah commits to the client.
- Once final approval is given, later changes should be handled as new support, maintenance, or additional project work unless Ric agrees otherwise.

## Launch Payment And Approval Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

- Client has approved final preview.
- Final payment received or payment agreement confirmed.
- Domain/hosting access ready.
- Contact form tested.
- Phone/email links checked.
- Mobile checked.
- Basic SEO checks complete.
- Backup/export saved where relevant.
- Handover notes prepared.

Tracker fields to update:

- Quote status
- Deposit/payment status
- Revision status
- Final approval status
- Launch readiness
- Handover status
